What happens to your order

1
Sealed in your browser
Only a fingerprint goes on-chain. A zero-knowledge proof shows the order is valid and paid for, without showing it.
2
Money locked, amount private
Funds sit in a confidential token. Nobody can see your size, so nobody can trade against you before the close.
3
One fair price at the close
All orders open at once. The contract finds the price where the most can trade, and everyone gets that same price.
4
Published as an oracle
Price (median of 7 auctions) and depth go on Stellar through the standard SEP-40 feed. Any pool or DEX can read them.
Why you can't fake depthDepth only counts buy orders locked for 7 days.If someone sells into their own order, that order is filled and leaves the book. Self-trading can only make depth smaller, never bigger.
